# Code of conduct

Pullwright follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Mystery01092000/pullwright/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of conduct", or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or ban contributors who do not follow these standards, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
